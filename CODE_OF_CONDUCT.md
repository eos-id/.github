# Community Standards

We aim to maintain a respectful, constructive, and safe environment for everyone participating in Enterprise-OS.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, inclusive, and considerate.
- Focus critique on ideas, evidence, and implementation—not people.
- Assume good intent while taking impact seriously.
- Share context, explain disagreements, and welcome different perspectives.
- Protect confidential information and respect privacy.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner or repository maintainers. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible, may seek additional context, and may take proportionate action—including editing or removing content, limiting participation, or restricting access. Retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith is not acceptable.

This policy applies in EOS project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project.
